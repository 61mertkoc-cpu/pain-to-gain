# PainToGain - Privacy Policy / Gizlilik Politikası

**Last updated / Son güncelleme:** 2026-07-22

## English

### 1. Overview
We at PainToGain ("the App") respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

### 2. Information We Collect
Our App does not require a mandatory registration system that collects personal identity (name, phone number, etc.). Your in-app progress and habit tracking data are stored locally on your device.

### 3. Advertising & Third-Party Services
The App is provided for free and uses Google AdMob advertising network to cover development costs. Google AdMob may collect anonymous advertising identifiers to display relevant ads. For more information, please visit [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 4. Contact Us
If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via email.
